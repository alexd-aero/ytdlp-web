    How it works

    1You tap the bookmark

    It runs a tiny helper on the YouTube page you're on and opens the floating card.

    2yt-dlp runs in the card

    The real yt-dlp (Python, compiled to WebAssembly) finds every stream. The helper fetches them as YouTube.

    3FFmpeg builds your file

    Merging, converting, cover art and metadata all happen in your browser. Then tap Download now.
